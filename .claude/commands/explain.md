Explain $ARGUMENTS the way you'd explain it to a senior Android interviewer
who is probing your design choices.

Cover, tightly:
1. What it does (1-2 sentences).
2. Why this approach, versus at least one credible alternative you didn't
   pick.
3. The trade-off you accepted by choosing it.
4. What would break, or need to change, if this had to scale (more users,
   more data, more screens depending on it).

Keep the whole answer to what you could plausibly say out loud in 60-90
seconds — not an essay. This is rehearsal for a real answer, not
documentation.
